PRIVACY/SECURITY RULES
- Never include question-paper plaintext, passwords, access tokens, AES keys, KEM shared secrets, private key bytes or credentials in event logs.
- Avoid unnecessary personally identifying data. Prefer pseudonymous actor IDs and agreed role labels.
- Use UTC timestamps in a stable format.
- A failed cryptographic verification is a fact/event; AI decides how it contributes to risk scores.
- AI alerts do not replace deterministic controls: invalid signatures, hash mismatches, unauthorized roles and pre-release attempts must be denied by the application regardless of any model output.
