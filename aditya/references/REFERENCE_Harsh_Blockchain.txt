ADITYA'S PROPOSED CRYPTO WORKFLOW
- ML-KEM-768: post-quantum key encapsulation/key establishment.
- HKDF-SHA-256: derives a 32-byte AES key from the KEM shared secret.
- AES-256-GCM: encrypts/authenticates the PDF.
- SHA3-256: calculates a digest over a precisely defined byte sequence (proposed: encrypted file bytes; potentially a separate canonical-manifest digest).
- ML-DSA-65: signs the canonical manifest, which binds the encrypted-file digest to exam metadata and cryptographic parameters.
- Encrypted file/manifest are intended for private S3 storage. No exam plaintext or private keys should be written on-chain.

IMPORTANT HASH COMPATIBILITY NOTE
- SHA3-256 and Ethereum's keccak256 are not interchangeable. Do not label keccak256 as SHA3-256.
- If the smart contract computes keccak256 for its own purposes, document that separately. The contract may simply store the off-chain SHA3-256 digest as bytes32 without recomputing it.
- Confirm whether the existing contract expects SHA-256, SHA3-256, Keccak-256, or a raw bytes32 digest. Do not silently change Harsh's contract expectation.
